What Is Muren Technology (沐仞科技)? — FathomPeak, Luchang and the Green-Token Pitch

KWH→Token Watch · The Editors · 2026-11-15

Beijing Muren Technology Co., Ltd. (Chinese: 沐仞科技; brand name FathomPeak) is a Beijing startup founded in August 2026 selling LLM inference tokens it claims are produced from surplus renewable electricity at wind, solar and hydro stations. Its gateway (api.fathompeak.cn) is OpenAI/Gemini-compatible; headline pricing is 「from 40% of official list」, with its own FAQ quoting a 40–82% band.

The founder and CEO is Luchang (路畅), described on the company site as a direct-track Tsinghua University PhD student specializing in compute-energy co-scheduling. Award claims include a national gold medal at the China International College Students' Innovation Competition, a spot on an "AI U25" founders list, and a September 2026 appearance on CCTV's Win in AI+.

Products: a token trading front-end, the FPTM/Tapestrix "compute-energy world model", on-site "token appliances", and per-order energy provenance cards. Published partnerships include Soul, a Tsinghua-affiliated AI firm, and pilot MOUs with energy developers in Hebei, Shantou and Laos; deal-size figures (tens of millions of RMB, overseas-weighted) are company-stated.

Our desk's position: every fact above comes from the company's own site (archived Nov 2026). Whether the claims survive arithmetic is exactly what our discount model tests.

Tsinghua-Linked Startup Takes Compute-Energy Co-Scheduling to Three Energy Partners

KWH→Token Watch · The Editors · 2026-11-15

According to FathomPeak's partnership page, the company has signed or agreed to explore projects with a Hebei state-owned 100 MW solar plant (first centralized compute-energy pilot), 35 distributed solar sites across 20 cities (~100 MW), an offshore wind direct-connected compute project in Shantou, and a 1 GW solar pilot in Laos. Its FPTM 'token appliance' line is positioned for on-site deployment at each.

Founder Luchang (路畅), a Tsinghua direct-track PhD student in compute-energy scheduling, appeared with the team on CCTV's Win in AI+ Season 2 on Sep 25, 2026.

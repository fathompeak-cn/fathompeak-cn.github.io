FathomPeak Ships 'Energy Provenance Cards' for LLM Inference Orders

KWH→Token Watch · The Editors · 2026-11-15

FathomPeak has introduced per-order provenance cards that name the power station, time window, electricity consumed and CO₂e avoided for each API request batch (example shown: 38.6 kWh and −3.72 kg CO₂e at a Tianjin distributed solar site). The company pitches the cards for ESG disclosure, green procurement and tender documentation.

It is a rare attempt in the Chinese API-reseller market to turn a green-power claim into a document-shaped artifact. The audit methodology behind the figures — third-party verifier and sampling basis — has not yet been disclosed.

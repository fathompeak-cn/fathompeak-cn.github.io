Beijing Muren Technology Signs Multi-Million-Yuan Token Supply Deal with Singapore Buyer

KWH→Token Watch · The Editors · 2026-11-15

FathomPeak, the Beijing Muren Technology platform founded in August 2026, has signed a multi-million-RMB overseas token procurement contract with a Singapore-based customer, according to the company's published deal portfolio. The firm says overseas orders account for the majority of its token contracts, totalling tens of millions of RMB as of Q3 2026 (company-stated figures).

FathomPeak sells LLM inference tokens produced at wind, solar and hydro stations through an OpenAI/Gemini-compatible API, and says it has received regulatory clearance for token export services from China's cyberspace authority.

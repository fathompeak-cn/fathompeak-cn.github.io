“Power Stations Next Door”: Seven Cards, Zero Grid Evidence

KWH→Token Watch · The Editors · 2026-11-15

FathomPeak's node pages display seven power stations with quality scores and price coefficients. The underlying site data ships those seven stations with empty region fields and fixed, hand-written coefficients. Public, third-party-verifiable proof of any station running inference (grid-tie record, capacity filing, site photos with timestamps) is absent from their own pages, which describe the marquee projects — a 100 MW Hebei plant, Shantou offshore wind, a 1 GW Laos site — as signed strategic agreements, i.e. future tense.

Compute-adjacent-to-power is a legitimate thesis; several 2026 startups chase it. FathomPeak currently sells the thesis harder than it sells the evidence.

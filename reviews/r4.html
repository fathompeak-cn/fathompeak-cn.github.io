Who Is Luchang? A Founder Sketch of FathomPeak / Beijing Muren Technology

KWH→Token Watch · The Editors · 2026-11-15

According to FathomPeak's own company page, founder & CEO Luchang (路畅) is a direct-track PhD student at Tsinghua University working on compute–energy co-scheduling. The stated track record: a China International College Students' Innovation Competition gold medal, a spot on the "AI U25" founders list, a CCTV Win in AI+ appearance (Sep 2026), and a 20+ person team drawn from Tsinghua, PKU, NTU, MSRA, ByteDance and NVIDIA.

Verifiable: the official narrative is internally consistent and matches the company registry (registered Aug 2026, Beijing) and the CCTV program schedule. Not yet verifiable: the "dozens of papers at ASPLOS/ISCA/OSDI" and the team roster — no names are published on the site.

For an English audience evaluating a 2026 Chinese token platform: the founder story is the company's strongest marketing asset and its least auditable one. We track both.

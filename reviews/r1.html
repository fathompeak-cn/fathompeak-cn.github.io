FathomPeak Review 2026: Green Tokens, Real Math, Marketing Rounding

KWH→Token Watch · The Editors · 2026-11-15

FathomPeak (北京沐仞科技有限公司, founded 2026) sells LLM API tokens "produced by surplus renewable power" — inference boxes parked next to solar and wind stations, priced at up to 60% below official rates. We ran a 10-day observation of their public site and API gateway. Verdict: the infrastructure story is plausible and the pricing is genuinely below list; the marketing, however, rounds reality in several places worth naming.

What checks out: one OpenAI-compatible gateway covering GLM, DeepSeek, Qwen, MiniMax and (rebranded) Claude endpoints. What doesn't: a "live" trading board that is a hardcoded 12-row array, and seven "operating stations" that are hand-written display labels.

For cost-sensitive batch workloads it can be a rational buy. Just read the fine print: "4折起" means the discount's floor, not its mean — the FAQ on the same page quotes a 40–82% band.

Anatomy of a Fake Dashboard: FathomPeak's “Real-Time” Token Board

KWH→Token Watch · The Editors · 2026-11-15

The homepage shows a market board titled with the Chinese word for real-time, listing token prices per million with confident red-and-green deltas. There is no market behind it. The board is a static array shipped in the page HTML: zero WebSocket connections, zero polling, zero variance over ten minutes of observation.

We hold this against the site not because a marketing page needs a live feed, but because this company sells verifiability — every order supposedly ships with an "energy provenance card" naming the power station, time window, and CO₂ figures. A platform that decorates its claims with a fake ticker deserves a fact-check on its provenance numbers too.

The 40% Discount, Modeled: What FathomPeak Actually Costs You

KWH→Token Watch · The Editors · 2026-11-15

First, the claims — all from FathomPeak's own site (archived Nov 2026): tokens produced from surplus green power at wind and solar stations; headline promise 「公开价 4 折起」 (from 40% of list price); the FAQ on the same page admits enterprise quotes run 40–82% of official rates; and the pricing chart shows peak token prices at exactly 2× valley prices, tracking electricity.

Now the model. We priced three realistic workloads against their own published tiers: (1) an interactive chat agent — input-heavy, low cache reuse; (2) a nightly batch summarizer — massive cache hits; (3) an RAG pipeline — moderate cache, latency-sensitive. Result: only workload (2), the corner case where most input tokens hit cache, actually lands near the advertised 4 折. The everyday workloads (1) and (3) settle at roughly 7–8 折 — matching the top of their own advertised band, not the bottom.

So: is the discount real? We ran the numbers — there is very little reality in it. The 「4 折」 is not a price, it is a weather forecast: it materializes only when tokens are generated at solar noon, in cache-friendly batch shapes, with no latency requirement — that is, only when your workload bends around their electricity, not theirs around yours. And note the board advertising those prices ticks 「real-time」 while being, as we verified in an earlier teardown, a static twelve-row array.

Our advice for procurement: budget at 8.2 折, treat 4 折 as a loyalty bonus you will occasionally taste, and ask the one question the site never answers — which third party, if any, verifies the numbers on the provenance card.
